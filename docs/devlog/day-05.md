# Day 5: Friday Oct. 30th, 2026

The fifth day built Get It Fixed, a picture of each fix, a second check and a drawing of the house, and made the first deploy ready.

## Built

- **Get It Fixed**: the homeowner enters a ZIP code and sees licensed crews from California's public contractor list for each kind of work, closest first, with miles, license status and a link to check the license. Work no crew covers is shown as Yours to Do. EmberWalk drafts each request, and nothing is sent until the homeowner approves it. An email goes with the proof photos, or the homeowner sends a text from the phone. Replies are read for a price, a date and any questions.
- **Defensible space record**: each finding is marked Needs Fixing, Fix Planned or Done, with its date, across every walk, newest first. A mark made with no signal is saved when signal returns. Once every Required Now item is Done, EmberWalk drafts an inspection request for the homeowner to copy and send.
- **See It Fixed**: a preview of the top three findings fixed, each on its own blurred still, shown under More and labelled as an AI preview, not a photo.
- **A second check**: a second model reviews each finding's photo against its rule, and when it sees it differently the finding shows "Second check disagrees:" with its reason.
- **The house drawn**: the house from above with rings at 5, 30 and 100 feet and a numbered pin for each finding, placed on the side it was seen from and at its measured distance. Each pin opens its finding.
- **Pans by corner**: each pan is tagged with its corner, and one corner can be filmed on its own.
- **Rules for the newer objects**: gas cans, stored fuel, lawn mowers, grills, small propane cylinders, vehicles, lumber piles, tarps, doormats and boats or trailers each get a rule at the distance a published fire safety source gives, such as 3 feet for a grill and 10 feet for stored fuel. Wooden decks within 5 feet of the house and chimney spark arresters are covered too.
- **The walk sent while recorded**: walks upload while they are being recorded and pick up again when signal returns, and Stop never waits on the network.
- **Send again, never walk again**: the phone keeps each walk's video, so a house is never walked twice.
- **More on the phone**: the EmberWalk mark on Home, a screen that stays awake while recording, and the last 40 proof photos opened, kept so they open with no signal. The Simple and Detailed switch sits on each finding as well as the results.
- **Ready for a first deploy**: the app at go.emberwalk.app, the site at emberwalk.app and a health check for every service. Each visitor can send 3 walks a day.
- **Sample houses**: conifers, oaks and wooden decks, and an after picture for each required fix.
