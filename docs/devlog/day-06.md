# Day 6: Saturday Oct. 31st, 2026

The sixth day finished the homeowner's report and the tools that keep a household's records private, added yearly plans, and readied the website. The website goes live shortly after this upload.

## Built

- **A report for the homeowner**: a PDF called Defensible Space Record. Each finding is grouped by zone, the first 5 feet, 5 to 30 feet and 30 to 100 feet, labelled Required Now or Recommended, and marked Needs Fixing, Fix Planned or Done, with its blurred proof photo and its dates. The homeowner approves the blurred photos first and can remove any finding that is not right; removed items are listed apart. No report is made until that approval exists.
- **A link to share the report**: a long random link that opens the approved report and nothing else, read only and hidden from search engines. Turn Off Link ends it at any time. If a photo or a mark changes after approval, the link waits until the homeowner approves the report again.
- **Delete Everything**: after one confirmation that says it cannot be undone, every walk, clip, proof photo, recheck photo, report, share link and Get It Fixed message a phone has made is erased, and the cost records keep no link back to the household.
- **A recheck photo**: one photo of a spot marked Done comes back as cleared, still there or unsure, and the report shows the date it was rechecked. Each item can be rechecked up to five times.
- **Walks that keep their good sides**: when one part of a walk cannot be checked, only the side that part alone covered is left open. Every other side keeps its results, and the phone asks for that side alone.
- **Plans**: yearly plans for homeowners, with 4 walks a year, and for landlords and property managers, with 15 walks a year counted per walk, not per building. Extra walks can be added in one purchase, and unused ones carry over. Sample walks and walks that could not be checked never count against a plan.
- **An owner page**: the day's model spend against the daily cap, the time each step takes, walks by stage, any walk that stopped and where, and emails sent and waiting for approval, all on one private page behind a sign in.
- **An accuracy scorer**: compares the findings with each sample house's answer key, counting the hazards found, the findings that were right and the zone each one landed in, and whether the fix first list comes out in the right order.
- **The website's pages**: Pricing, Terms of Service, Privacy, Refunds, About, Contact, Security and Accessibility. The Accessibility page names WCAG 2.2 AA as its target, and the Security page explains that the site sets no cookies, loads no analytics and asks for no account or password.
- **Records kept 12 months**: a household's records are deleted 12 months after its last walk when no plan is current, checked every hour, unless the household deletes them sooner.
- **The hero house**: the main sample house, built in Blender from photoscanned plants, ground and materials, now plays as the phone's sample walk under See a Sample House, labelled as a synthetic sample.
