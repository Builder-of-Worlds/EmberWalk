# Day 8: Monday Nov. 2nd, 2026

The eighth day turned on every Spanish line, added the yearly renewal notice, and reworked the home page around the plans. It also made the distance of each finding more careful where an object meets the house.

## Built

- **Spanish everywhere**: every Spanish line now shows on a phone set to Spanish, with people described in gender neutral words and one word for renewal throughout. The longest new Spanish account screens fit a 390 pixel phone.
- **A yearly renewal notice**: before a plan renews, the homeowner gets an email naming the renewal date and the yearly amount, with a link to cancel. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and the walks stay until the end of the year paid for. A refund in full of a plan also cancels its renewal.
- **Our advice, not a rule**: where the cited rule does not give the detail an instruction needs, the line is marked Our advice, not a rule, on the phone, on the map and in the report, in both languages.
- **A home page built around the plans**: the opening photo fills a desktop screen and dissolves as the next section rises over it. Plans and prices sit on the home page, all seven steps of a walk are laid out in order, and running paragraphs read left aligned in a centered column. The Pricing page carries a launch line.
- **Plainer pages**: the Accessibility page is in plain words. The Privacy page says what Delete Everything does to an account, signed in or not, and covers the crew job page and the app's one cookie. The location line says what EmberWalk does: no aerial photo and no map provider.
- **Closer to the house, told plainly**: a finding that may sit inside the first 5 feet is counted with the items closest to the house. An object touching the wall reads Against the house, and so does a pile leaning on it. An object is measured against the wall behind it, and is never called 0 feet away without being seen touching.
- **Cleaner screens and messages**: the demo inbox and crew replies keep their line breaks, crew lists and drafts no longer repeat a sentence, the status no longer says Done twice, and the record screen's way back says where it goes. The sample recheck names its sample photo.
- **The judges' portal in two languages**: the hazard sentences follow the language chosen, and the sample walk's corner lines match the walk itself.
