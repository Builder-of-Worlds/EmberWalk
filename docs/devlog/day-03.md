# Day 3: Wednesday Oct. 28th, 2026

The third day brought a walk back to the phone as results, confirmed that a walk covered the whole house, and made every instruction plain.

## Built

- **Results on the phone**: after a walk is sent, the phone checks on it every 2 seconds, says it is being checked and opens the results when the check is done.
- **Redo one side**: when a side was missed, the phone asks for that side alone, with one button per side and a tip on how to film it. The homeowner is asked for the whole walk again only when all four sides are missing.
- **Coverage check**: the pipeline confirms the walk went all the way around by the phone's compass, ended where it started, and showed the base of the wall on every side. A side that was not walked, or whose wall base never shows, comes back to the phone as a gap with a hint.
- **Location**: the phone picks the fire rules from where the walk happens. It asks for location the way it asks for the camera, never waits for the answer, and works out the rules on the phone, so no coordinate is stored or sent.
- **Fix first list**: the three nearest open findings within 30 feet of the house, nearest first, measured from the wall, so the homeowner knows where to start.
- **Plain wording**: every rule, in English and Spanish, says what to do and offers a real way to do it, such as metal or brick for a fence or where to move a wood pile. The text avoids legal code words and urgency words, and the color of a finding shows how urgent it is.
- **Problem reports**: the phone keeps the walk, asks again every 30 seconds and sends one report with nothing about the person in it. Once the report is received, the screen says "EmberWalk was told about the problem. This page updates by itself."
- **Video sweep**: every hour, walk videos older than 24 hours are deleted, so no video is kept longer than a day.
- **Object catalog**: 25 things to look for, among them gas cans, stored fuel, lawn mowers, grills, vehicles, lumber piles, tarps, doormats and boats or trailers. Each has one plain reason it matters in a fire and a dated source.
- **Voice notes**: a homeowner can talk while filming. Spoken notes are written out and attached to the finding they describe, shown under What You Said. The sound is deleted with the video.
- **Get It Fixed requests**: each quote request lists the exact work with a blurred photo per item. A crew that has not replied after 3 days gets a follow up, drafted for the homeowner to approve. Once every Required Now item is done, an inspection request with CAL FIRE's links is drafted.
