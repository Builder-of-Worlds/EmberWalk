# Day 7: Sunday Nov. 1st, 2026

The seventh day brought Spanish to every phone screen, gave homeowners and landlords a way to sign in, and opened Get It Fixed to local crews and fire departments. It also put the sample houses, a results map and a portal for the judges in place.

## Built

- **English or Español on every screen**: a choice on each phone screen, kept on the phone, with every rule line written in both languages. The landing page, the signup email and the homeowner's report read in Spanish too, and the crew form, Hear It, Help Near You, Sign In, Your Homes, Invite a Tenant and the inspection request all have their Spanish words.
- **Sign in, when it helps**: a homeowner can sign in by a link sent to their email, with no password, and their walks and plan follow them to a new phone. Walking a house never needs an account. Sign Out clears the phone, and Delete Everything works whether the phone is signed in or not.
- **Landlords and property managers**: a manager signs in by email link, adds properties, and makes an invite link for a tenant to walk a home. Your Homes lists every property, and each property's dated record comes out as one PDF. A read only sample portfolio shows how it looks.
- **Get It Fixed with local crews**: crews list themselves for free, and a homeowner can name a crew they already use. A message to a crew opens a private crew page with the job, and every text waits for the homeowner's approval before it goes. Help Near You and Hear It work on the sample houses with a demo inbox.
- **Fire departments in one tap**: where a fire department lists an inspection address, the homeowner can email it in one tap, and the inspection request screen links to the CAL FIRE form.
- **A results map**: findings show as pins around the house, with corner pins kept legible when they crowd, and the screen says whether a ZIP code is covered. A walk is dated by the phone's own day.
- **Sample houses**: the mobile home and apartment walks are finished in the same photoscanned look as the main house, and the main house has its final look, with a steady walk that turns slowly and keeps the camera level.
- **One finding for one object**: an object seen from two sides or two corners now shows as one finding, placed where it was best seen. Every finding of a large walk gets its written instruction.
- **The judges' portal**: built as a site of its own for judges.emberwalk.app, with the sample walk, the full result and a demo of the app, streaming the walk in full detail; it opens once its final media are in.
- **Clearer rule lines**: the propane tank and shed lines say 10 feet out on every side, as the California rule does, in English and Spanish.

## Numbers

The full test run at the day's last landing, Sunday Nov. 1st, 2026: 7,123 tests, all passing. Day 6 ended at 6,263.
