# Refund and cancellation policy
Description: When a refund stops a plan or voids extra walks, how to ask for one, and how renewal and cancellation work.
Last updated: 2026-10-31

This page covers refunds and cancellation for EmberWalk plans and extra walks. The prices are on the [Pricing](/pricing) page.

## A refund on a plan

A full refund of a plan stops new walks on that plan. It stops only the plan period that charge paid for, so a plan you buy after a refund works as normal. If we refund a plan, we also cancel its renewal.

## A refund on extra walks

Any refund on an extra walk purchase, partial or full, voids that whole purchase.

## Failed walks

A walk that fails does not count against your walks, and you can try it again until it completes.

## How to ask for a refund

You can ask for a full refund of a plan within 14 days of a charge for it. You can ask for a refund of extra walks within 14 days of the charge if you have not used any of them.

Write to us from the email address on your Stripe receipt and say which charge it is about.

{{contact}}

We reply by email and start the refund when we approve it. Your bank usually shows it 5 to 10 business days later.

## Renewal

Your plan renews on its own every year on the day you bought it, at the price on the [Pricing](/pricing) page, until you cancel.

About 30 days before each renewal, we email you the renewal date, the price and how to cancel. If the price changes, that email tells you.

## Cancelling

You can cancel any time with the manage plan link in your Stripe receipt or renewal email, or by writing to us at the address above. Cancelling stops the next renewal.

You keep your walks until the end of the year you paid for. After the 14 day refund window, the rest of a paid year is not refunded.
