Privacy policy

What we collect

When you sign up, you give us two things: your email address, and whether you are a homeowner, a landlord or property manager, or something else. We also keep the date and time you signed up. We do not ask for your name, your address, your phone number or your location.

Why we collect it

We use your email address to tell you when EmberWalk launches and to send you the confirmation email. We use your answer to understand who EmberWalk is for. We'll only email you about EmberWalk.

What the app keeps on your phone

The EmberWalk app has no account, name or password. It keeps these on your phone:

  • A random code that names your walks to our servers. It is the only way we tell your walks from anyone else's.
  • Your recordings, video and sound, until the check of that walk ends.
  • The results and pictures of your three newest walks, so they open again later.
  • Your marks on findings (planned or done, a note, a date) and your settings.
  • The app's own files, so it opens without a connection.

What the app sends

  • The video and sound of each walk, in parts, to our storage at Cloudflare.
  • A rule area key such as ca. The phone reads its position once, at low accuracy, keeps only the key and sends only the key. Your coordinates are never sent.
  • The camera's name and size, and compass, tilt and light readings taken during the walk. The light reading is the average brightness of a tiny camera frame.
  • The language you use and the time of the walk.
  • If a send fails and you report it, the walk's id, a refusal code and the app version.

None of our code stores your IP address or browser details. Cloudflare, which hosts us, may keep its own request logs.

Video, sound and voice notes

  • Video is kept only while your walk is checked, and deleted when the walk finishes. A sweep every hour also deletes any original video still there after 24 hours.
  • The app records sound by default, so you can speak notes while you walk. The sound is cut out, sent for transcription and deleted straight after. The words it heard are kept with the walk until the video is deleted. Words matched to a finding are kept with that finding as a voice note until you use Delete Everything.

Pictures and findings

  • The findings, proof pictures and other results made from your walk are kept until you use Delete Everything.
  • Proof pictures are blurred where EmberWalk finds people, faces, license plates, house numbers, street signs or mailboxes. Photos you take later to recheck a fix are not blurred. They are kept until you use Delete Everything.
  • Your marks on findings, with any note and the date you marked them done, are kept the same way.

Get It Fixed

If you use Get It Fixed to ask licensed crews for help, EmberWalk keeps the ZIP code you type, any crew email address you type, the messages it drafts, the approvals you give with the pictures attached, your follow up notes and any reply text you log.

Crews come from the state's public license lists, not from you. Nothing goes out without your okay: EmberWalk emails a crew only after you approve that message. A crew that has only a phone number gets a link that opens your own Messages app, and EmberWalk sends nothing for it.

When a crew answers by email, the answer comes in through Cloudflare Email Routing, and only from a crew already emailed on that job. The sender, subject and text are kept. Attachments are dropped. If a crew asks to be removed, its address and license go on a list with no end date, so that crew is never emailed again.

Payments

Plans and extra walks are paid for on a page Stripe hosts. EmberWalk never sees or stores your card number. When you pay, EmberWalk gives Stripe the random code from your phone so the payment can be matched to your phone.

EmberWalk keeps Stripe's customer and subscription ids, your plan status and renewal date, the price you paid, how many extra walks you have bought and used, and which charges were refunded.

Who handles it

Your email address and answer are stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site. The confirmation email is sent through Resend. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person, and not a program, is using the sign up form; it runs in your browser when you use the form. These companies handle your information only to do those jobs for us.

Who handles what the app sends

For the app, these companies handle your information to do the jobs we give them:

  • Cloudflare hosts the app's servers, the database, the file storage and the email routing.
  • Resend sends the crew emails you approve in Get It Fixed, with the crew's address, the text you approved, a link to opt out, EmberWalk's footer and the blurred pictures you approved as attachments.
  • Meta runs the models that check your walk. It receives each clip of the walk without a blur, and still pictures cut from the walk, some without a blur and some blurred. It also receives the walk's sound for transcription, the transcript and the findings to write your list, your recheck photos and, for Get It Fixed, the trade, ZIP code and findings and each crew reply. Pictures reach Meta before the blur is applied. Our requests tell Meta not to store them, and the files we upload are set to expire after one hour and are deleted when the walk ends. What Meta does beyond that is set by Meta's own terms.
  • Baseten can run a second check on a finding, with its blurred proof picture. It is switched off for real walks today.
  • Stripe takes your payments, as above.

We never sell it

We never sell your information, and we never share it for advertising.

Cookies, tracking and Do Not Track

This site sets no cookies of its own and has no advertising and no analytics. We do not track you across other websites, so a Do Not Track signal from your browser changes nothing here. The EmberWalk app sets no cookies and has no analytics either.

Cloudflare Turnstile sees your IP address and browser details, and may set a strictly necessary cookie, only to check you are a person. No other company tracks you across sites through EmberWalk.

How long we keep things

  • The sign up list: until you use the unsubscribe link in an email, which deletes your row.
  • Video: until the walk finishes, and never past 24 hours. Sound: deleted straight after it is transcribed.
  • Findings, proof pictures, recheck photos, marks and Get It Fixed records: until you use Delete Everything. If you never use Delete Everything, we delete your findings, proof pictures, recheck photos, marks and Get It Fixed records 12 months after your last walk or the end of your last paid plan, whichever is later.
  • Fault reports that sit for 30 days are deleted when the next report arrives.
  • Cost records of the model calls, saved model answers and the list of crews that asked to be removed: with no end date.

Delete Everything

In the app, Delete Everything erases what is stored under your phone's code: the video leftovers, clips, proof pictures, recheck photos, findings, marks, Get It Fixed jobs, messages and crew replies. It is refused while a walk is still being checked. Afterwards your phone gets a new code and clears its results, pictures and marks. Recordings you never sent stay on the phone. It also unlinks your plan from your phone's code.

Delete Everything does not erase:

  • cost records of the model calls, which are kept without the link to your walk
  • model answers saved without a walk
  • the list of crews that asked not to be emailed again
  • your plan and payment records
  • the sign up list, which the unsubscribe link in every email removes
  • the run history Cloudflare keeps for each walk
  • emails already delivered, and anything Meta or Baseten kept on their side

How to be removed

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. Using it removes you straight away, with nothing to sign in to. You can also write to hello@emberwalk.app from the address you signed up with and ask to be removed. We will delete your email address and your answer from the list. To see what we hold about you, or to correct it, write to the same address.

Children

EmberWalk is not meant for children under 13. If you tell us a child signed up, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this page, we will change the date below.
