When the next wildfire comes, will your home be ready?

According to CAL FIRE, up to 90 percent of homes that catch fire in a wildfire are set alight by embers. Embers ride the wind and land in the dry leaves, the wood pile by the wall, the fence that touches the siding. The small things around your home can decide what happens to it, and most people have no idea which ones matter.

EmberWalk does.

Take one walk around your house with your phone. EmberWalk shows you exactly what could catch, what to fix first, and what California actually requires, so you're not left guessing.
