EmberWalk

Pricing

A walk is one filmed loop around a home. Every walk needs a plan, and a plan is paid for through Stripe. A plan renews on its own each year until you cancel; the refund and cancellation policy says how.

Individuals

$12.99 a year. 4 walks a year of your home.

Landlords and property managers

$39.99 a year. 15 walks a year at any buildings. Walks are counted, not buildings.

Extra walks

Either plan can buy extra walks at $2.99 each, up to 15 in one purchase.

Unused extra walks carry over into the next plan year, on either plan. When a plan renews, the new year's plan walks are used before any carried over extra walks.

What counts as a walk

A walk that fails does not count against your walks. You can try it again until it completes, at no cost to your plan.

What each plan includes

Every plan includes the findings from each walk with the rule behind each one, blurred proof pictures, recheck photos that update a finding, Get It Fixed, and Delete Everything. Only a walk uses up one of your walks; recheck photos and Get It Fixed do not.

Payment, refunds and terms

You pay through Stripe. Refunds and cancellation are in the refund and cancellation policy. The rules for using EmberWalk are in the Terms of Service.
